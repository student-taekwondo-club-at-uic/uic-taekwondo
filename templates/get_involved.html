Join us

No experience needed! Everyone is welcome, from total beginners to black belts.

  2. Scroll down to Taekwondo -> Registration -> You are engaged!!!
  3. Follow us on Instagram for updates.
  4. Come to a practice, Monday - Wednesday, 19:00 - 21:00 at The MAC Suite.
