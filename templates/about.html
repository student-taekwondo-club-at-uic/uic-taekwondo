About us

Who are we?

  • Student Taekwondo Club at UIC is a student organization at the University of Illinois at Chicago.
  • We are a group of students who share a passion for Taekwondo and martial arts.
  • We welcome students of all skill levels, from beginners to experienced practitioners.

What do we practice?

  • Sparring: training reflexes, endurance, and competition skills.
  • Poomsae: training techniques, precision, and the spirit of martial arts.
  • Conditioning: training strength, flexibility, and overall fitness, board breaking.

Training schedule

  • MONDAY - WEDNESDAY: 19:00PM - 21:00PM at The MAC Suite (UIC Gym)
  • We also participate in tournaments and events throughout the year.
